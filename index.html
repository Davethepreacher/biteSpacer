The BiteSpacer Method

Eat slowly, lose weight naturally.

This app helps you master mindful eating through 3 simple rules:

🍴
Savor each bite

Keep food in your mouth for at least 10 seconds. Chew well and enjoy the taste.

⏱️
Space your bites

Wait at least 15 seconds between each bite. The app guides you with the red light.

🎯
Respect meal duration

A meal should last 20–25 minutes. The timer turns orange at 20 min, red at 25 min.

Why does it work?

Satiety naturally kicks in after 15–20 minutes. By slowing down, you'll feel full with less food — no willpower needed.

Start with one meal. Notice how you feel after. Then try a full day, then a week. The results will speak for themselves.
